# Blackridge Mortgage Regime Case

## Prompt
Reconstruct the risk model before and after a mortgage-market regime change. Calculate historical VaR and CVaR for the initial and updated periods, explain how changing covariance altered portfolio tail risk, and evaluate the consequences of liquidating the mortgage book at synthetic fire-sale discounts.

## Data
- `mortgage_pool_holdings.csv`
- `mortgage_pool_returns.csv`
- `external_market_proxy.csv`

All numbers and instruments in the files are synthetic. Historical sources inform the scenario but do not supply the generated return observations or liquidation discounts.

## Deliverable
A completed workbook or notebook plus a 750–1,000 word risk-committee memorandum.
