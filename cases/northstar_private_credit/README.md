# Northstar Direct Lending Fund III

A fictional private-debt base case for the Risk Management Decision Lab.

## Core decision

Northstar must decide how to respond to an amendment request from Meridian Cloud Systems, a fictional software borrower. The case combines borrower credit deterioration, PIK interest, covenant relief, recovery uncertainty, fund liquidity, valuation opacity, and portfolio concentration.

## Intended experience

1. Committee briefing
2. Evidence room
3. Initial amendment decision
4. Scenario reveal
5. Recalculation of expected loss, recovery, leverage, interest coverage, and liquidity
6. Final decision and reversal condition
7. Board defense

## Synthetic-data notice

Every company, fund, figure, probability, and outcome is fictional. Public market commentary informed the themes only. The case does not describe any real transaction.
