    Applied Risk Management Lab

    Overview

    This platform provides a structured, data-driven analysis of major financial failures.

    Rather than describing risk conceptually, the lab emphasizes measurement, modeling, and failure analysis through real-world cases.

    Each case combines: - Conceptual framing
    - Structured assignments
    - Computational analysis
    - Evidence-based interpretation

    Featured Insights

    The following cases highlight distinct failure mechanisms in modern financial systems, progressing from model risk to systemic interaction:

    LTCM — Model Risk and Correlation Breakdown

    Statistical models can fail when their assumptions about volatility and correlation no longer hold.

    • Identifies how regime shifts invalidate historical calibration
    • Demonstrates how correlations converge under stress
    • Shows how leverage amplifies model error

    View LTCM Analysis →

    Lehman Brothers — Liquidity and Confidence Collapse

    Financial institutions fail when they lose funding access—not when capital first appears insufficient.

    • Compares regulatory capital vs balance-sheet vulnerability
    • Explains liquidity vs solvency distinction
    • Analyzes timeline-driven confidence erosion

    View Lehman Analysis →

    London Whale — VaR Failure and Tail Risk

    Measuring the likelihood of loss is not the same as understanding its magnitude.

    • Compares Value-at-Risk (VaR) to Expected Shortfall (ES)
    • Illustrates tail risk through simulation and visualization
    • Highlights limitations of standard risk metrics under stress

    View London Whale Analysis →

    Knight Capital — Operational and Model Risk Failure

    Operational failures in automated systems can generate catastrophic financial losses within minutes.

    • Demonstrates how faulty code deployment triggered millions of unintended trades
    • Accumulated ~$7B in unintended positions and ~$440M in losses within 45 minutes
    • Highlights failures in change control, testing, and model governance (SR 11‑7)
    • Shows how market impact and forced liquidation amplify losses
    • Illustrates how operational risk can become immediate financial risk in high-speed trading environments

    View Knight Capital Analysis →

    Global Financial Crisis — Systemic Risk Interaction

    Systemic crises occur when multiple risk models fail simultaneously.

    • Highlights divergence between CDS-implied risk and historical default assumptions
    • Demonstrates how structural credit signals (Z-score) reveal hidden fragility
    • Shows how correlation and portfolio losses scale under stress
    • Illustrates how CVA and counterparty exposure amplify market shocks
    • Captures how wrong-way risk and downgrade triggers create liquidity spirals

    View GFC Analysis →

    Sunrise National Bank — Stress Testing and Scenario Analysis

    Stress testing reveals vulnerabilities that are not visible under baseline or historical conditions.

    • Demonstrates how capital adequacy diverges under forward-looking Basel III scenarios
    • Shows how credit losses dominate total losses under stress conditions
    • Highlights how market and liquidity shocks amplify capital deterioration
    • Illustrates how supervisory scenarios can breach regulatory CET1 minimums
    • Uses reverse stress testing to identify viability thresholds and failure boundaries

    View SNB Analysis → (/Risk-Management/cases/snb_summary.md/)

    Atlas Bank International — Basel Regulation and Risk Transformation

    Regulatory frameworks shape how risk is measured, capitalized, and ultimately managed within financial institutions.

    • Shows how Basel I enabled regulatory arbitrage through coarse risk weights
    • Demonstrates how Basel II internal models underestimated risk under calm conditions
    • Reveals how VaR failed to capture extreme losses during the financial crisis
    • Illustrates how Basel II.5 introduced stressed risk measures (sVaR, IRC, CRM)
    • Highlights how Basel III reshaped capital, leverage, and liquidity constraints

    View ABI Analysis → (/Risk-Managementcases/abi_summary.md/)

    Framework

    Across all cases, the lab applies a consistent analytical approach:

    1. Measure risk using appropriate quantitative tools
    2. Evaluate model performance under changing conditions
    3. Identify failure mechanisms
    4. Develop risk management implications

    Case Workflow

    Each case is structured as a self-contained module:

    1. Review the Overview
    2. Complete the Assignment
    3. Work through the Lab Notebook
    4. Submit structured responses (Q1–Q4 / Q1–Q5)

    Learning Objective

    Develop the ability to:

    • Diagnose failures in financial risk systems
    • Evaluate limitations of quantitative models
    • Connect data, models, and institutional behavior
    • Formulate actionable risk management recommendations

    Platform Structure

    This lab integrates:

    • Applied finance theory
    • Python-based analysis
    • Interactive notebook workflows
    • Case-based learning design

    Intended Use

    This platform is designed for both:

    • Instructional use in applied finance courses
    • Professional demonstration of risk analysis frameworks
