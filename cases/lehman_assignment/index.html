    Capital, Confidence, and Failure — The Lehman Brothers Case

    Context

    On September 15, 2008, Lehman Brothers filed for bankruptcy—the largest in U.S. history.

    At the time, the firm reported capital ratios that appeared comparable to its peers. Yet it collapsed within days due to a sudden loss of market confidence.

    This case examines a central risk management question:

    Why was capital insufficient to prevent failure?

    Instructions

    Complete the Lehman Risk Analysis Lab notebook.

    For each question (Q1–Q4): - Run the relevant analysis in the notebook
    - Reference the required outputs below
    - Provide concise, structured responses

    ⚠️ All responses must explicitly reference notebook outputs where indicated.

    🔷 Q1 — Capital Measures

    ✅ Expected Outputs

    • Chart: “Lehman: Tier 1 vs Capital Proxy”
    • Observed values:
    • Tier 1 ≈ 11% (Q2–Q3 2008)
    • Capital proxy rising as leverage falls

    ✅ Response Requirements

    • Explain the difference between:
    • Tier 1 capital
    • Equity-to-assets (capital proxy)
    • Explain why they diverge
    • Identify which better reflects vulnerability

    🔷 Q2 — Liquidity vs Solvency

    ✅ Expected Outputs

    • Conceptual content from notebook:
    • Solvency vs liquidity
    • Funding dynamics
    • Repo / collateral systems

    ✅ Response Requirements

    • Define solvency and liquidity
    • Explain why capital was considered “not very relevant”
    • Describe how funding runs destroy solvency

    🔷 Q3 — Timeline and Confidence

    ✅ Expected Outputs

    • Timeline events:
    • June 2008 loss
    • September 10 announcement
    • September 12–14 weekend

    ✅ Response Requirements

    • Identify 2 key confidence breakdown moments
    • Explain role of uncertainty
    • Explain why no rescue occurred

    🔷 Q4 — Policy and Governance

    ✅ Expected Outputs

    • Must reference results from:
    • Q1 (capital divergence)
    • Q2 (liquidity dynamics)
    • Q3 (timeline)

    ✅ Response Requirements

    Choose one:

    Option 1 — Risk Management

    • Propose 2 concrete balance sheet or funding changes

    Option 2 — Regulatory Design

    • Propose 1 improvement to stress testing or capital rules

    🔷 Final Synthesis

    ✅ Expected Outputs

    Students must reference: - Capital divergence (Q1) - Liquidity dynamics (Q2) - Confidence collapse (Q3)

    ✅ Response

    Explain:

    Why was capital alone insufficient to prevent Lehman’s collapse?

    Your response must integrate both quantitative and conceptual evidence.
