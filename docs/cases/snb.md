# Sunrise National Bank (SNB)

---

## Overview

The Sunrise National Bank (SNB) case examines how financial institutions assess resilience under stress using forward-looking scenario analysis.

Rather than relying solely on historical data, this case evaluates how the bank’s capital position evolves under severe but plausible shocks to credit, market, liquidity, and counterparty conditions.

Pre-computed results include:

- Scenario-specific losses across portfolios  
- Post-stress CET1 capital ratios  
- Interactive visualizations  
- Reverse stress testing identifying viability thresholds  

---

## Analytical Focus

- Scenario-based stress testing under Basel III  
- Capital adequacy and CET1 deterioration  
- Loss composition across portfolios  
- Reverse stress testing and viability boundaries  
- Interaction between credit, market, and liquidity risk  

---

## Analytical Lens

**Insight:** Stress testing reveals vulnerabilities that are not observable under baseline conditions.

**Quant Takeaway:** Under stress scenarios, SNB’s CET1 ratio declines significantly (e.g., ≈4.8%, ≈3.1%, ≈4.3%), with supervisory scenarios breaching regulatory minimums. **Model Implication:** Risk management must incorporate forward-looking scenario analysis and interaction effects rather than relying on static capital metrics.
