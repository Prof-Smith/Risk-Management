# Sunrise National Bank (SNB) — Stress Testing Assignment

---

## Instructions

Complete the **SNB Stress Testing Lab notebook**.

For each question (Q1–Q4):

- Interpret the results provided in the notebook  
- Use scenario outputs and visualizations  
- Provide structured, evidence-based responses  

⚠️ This case focuses on **interpretation, not computation**.

---

## Context

You are a risk management analyst preparing materials for SNB’s Board Risk Committee.

The bank is evaluated under multiple stress scenarios that capture:

- Macroeconomic shocks  
- Market dislocation  
- Liquidity stress  
- Counterparty exposure  

Your objective is to identify vulnerabilities and recommend actions.

---

# 🔷 Q1 — Scenario Interpretation

For each scenario:

### ✅ Requirements

- Describe primary macro, market, and liquidity shocks  
- Identify knock-on effects (e.g., spread widening, liquidity spirals)  
- Evaluate portfolio-level impacts  
- Interpret resulting CET1 levels  

---

# 🔷 Q2 — Capital and Loss Analysis

Using the visualizations:

### ✅ CET1 Analysis

- Compare capital deterioration across scenarios  
- Identify which scenario poses the greatest solvency risk  
- Explain why  

---

### ✅ Loss Composition

- Identify dominant loss drivers  
- Explain why the supervisory scenario produces the largest losses  
- Discuss the role of credit, trading, and counterparty exposures  

---

# 🔷 Q3 — Comparative Scenario Assessment

Provide a structured comparison:

### ✅ Requirements

- Which scenario generates the largest losses?  
- Which risk factors are most impactful?  
- Which scenario SNB is most exposed to  
- Whether SNB remains compliant with Basel III capital requirements  

---

# 🔷 Q4 — Reverse Stress Testing

Using the heatmap:

### ✅ Requirements

- Identify the viability boundary  
- Determine combinations of shocks that breach CET1 requirements  
- Explain why liquidity deterioration accelerates failure  

---

# 🔷 Final Synthesis

Explain:

> How does stress testing reveal risks that traditional models fail to capture?

Use:

- Scenario results  
- Capital impacts
