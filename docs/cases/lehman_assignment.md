# Capital, Confidence, and Failure — The Lehman Brothers Case

---

## Context

On September 15, 2008, Lehman Brothers filed for bankruptcy—the largest in U.S. history.

At the time, the firm reported capital ratios that appeared comparable to its peers. Yet it collapsed within days due to a sudden loss of market confidence.

This case examines a central risk management question:

> Why was capital insufficient to prevent failure?
