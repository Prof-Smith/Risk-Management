# Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) — Integrated Risk Assessment

---

## Executive Summary

The ERM capstone integrates all major forms of financial risk—market, credit, liquidity, and operational—into a single, unified framework for analyzing firm-wide vulnerability.

While individual risk models provide insight into specific exposures, this case demonstrates how risks interact, amplify each other, and ultimately determine a bank’s overall stability.

---

## Core Problem

- Risk is often managed in silos (market, credit, liquidity, operational)  
- Individual models fail to capture cross-risk interactions  
- Liquidity and operational shocks can amplify market and credit losses  
- Fragmented analysis obscures firm-wide vulnerability  

---

## Analytical Approach

This case synthesizes multiple risk frameworks:

- **Market Risk:** VaR, Expected Shortfall, Monte Carlo simulations  
- **Credit Risk:** PD, LGD, EAD, Expected Loss  
- **Operational Risk:** Poisson–Lognormal simulation with heavy-tailed losses  
- **Liquidity Risk:** Maturity ladder and survival horizon  
- **Integrated Stress Testing:** Interaction across risk categories  
- **ERM Dashboard:** Severity × likelihood framework for decision-making 

---

## Key Findings

- Market risk produces tail losses exceeding $10M under stress scenarios 
- Credit exposures generate expected losses exceeding $400M across segments 
- Operational risk exhibits heavy-tailed losses with extreme outcomes exceeding $1.5B 
- Liquidity outflows exceed $30B in short-term stress scenarios, with limited survival horizon 
- Integrated risk exposure is significantly larger than any individual risk component  

---

## Key Insight

> The greatest risks to financial institutions arise not from individual exposures, but from the interaction of multiple risk types under stress.

---

## Implications for Risk Management

- Risk must be evaluated at the **enterprise level**, not in isolation  
- Stress testing must integrate multiple risk channels  
- Liquidity and operational risks can be dominant drivers in crisis conditions  
- Risk dashboards must support **decision-making at the board level**  

---

## Position Within Risk Framework

- LTCM → Model failure
- Lehman → Liquidity failure
- London Whale → Measurement failure
- GFC → Systemic interaction
- Knight → Operational and model risk failure
- SNB → Forward-looking risk design
- ABI → Regulatory framework
- **ERM → Integrated enterprise risk (capstone)**

---

## Capstone Role

This case synthesizes all prior cases to answer:

- How risks interact across a financial institution  
- How stress propagates across portfolios  
- How senior leadership should respond
