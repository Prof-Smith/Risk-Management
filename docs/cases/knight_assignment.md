## Context

Knight Capital’s trading glitch illustrates how failures in software, governance, and model risk management can lead to rapid and catastrophic losses.

You are tasked with analyzing the incident as if reporting to senior risk management.

---

# 🔷 Q1 — Incident Analysis and SR 11‑7 Diagnosis

### ✅ Requirements

- Summarize the event (150–250 words)  
- Identify at least five SR 11‑7 failures  
- For each failure:
  - Explain the requirement  
  - Describe how Knight violated it  
  - State the consequence
