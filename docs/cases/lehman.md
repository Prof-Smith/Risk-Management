# Lehman Brothers (2008)

---

## Case Overview

Lehman Brothers provides a canonical example of a **confidence-driven collapse**.

Unlike LTCM, which highlights model failure, Lehman demonstrates how:

- Capital can appear sufficient  
- Yet liquidity can disappear rapidly  
- And confidence can collapse abruptly  

---

## Why This Case Matters

Lehman’s failure highlights a central insight:

> Risk is not just about solvency — it is about funding and belief.

Even with:
- reported Tier 1 capital near 11%
- declining leverage ratios  

The firm collapsed due to:
- loss of funding
- balance sheet uncertainty
- failure of counterparties to extend credit
